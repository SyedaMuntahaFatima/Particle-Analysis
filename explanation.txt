Explanation of Results
1. Raising the threshold from 15 GeV to 20 GeV reduced the number of selected values from 5 to 3 because fewer particles have energies above a higher cut.
2. The histogram of the original data does not depend on the threshold, so changing the threshold does not affect the bars of the histogram; only the selection changes.
3. With 5 bins, the histogram is coarse and shows the overall spread of particle energies, while 10 bins divide the data into smaller intervals and show more detail.
4. With only ten energy values, the histogram with 10 bins contains many empty bins or bins with only one entry, so it does not show a smooth distribution.
5. With thousands of particle-energy values, the histogram would show a more meaningful distribution, and finer binning would help reveal more details about the particle energies.
